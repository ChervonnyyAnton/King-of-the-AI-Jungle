# 🌴 Skill: Jungle Prompter

**Purpose:** Helps the Workshop Participant generate perfectly tailored AI prompts based on their **Jungle Title** (Competence × Expertise).

> **Trigger:** Use this skill whenever the participant is unsure what prompt to paste into their AI Chat, or wants to customize a prompt for a specific task.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **Ask the participant** for their **Jungle Title** (e.g., "Trail Explorer", "Mechanic", "King of the Jungle").
2. **Cross-reference** the 2-Axis Path Selector to determine their exact role and AI maturity level.
3. **Generate** a ready-to-copy YAML prompt block that matches their specific needs.
4. **Attach a "How to use this block"** helper line for clarity (especially for Beginner AI levels).

## 📝 Output Format

Always return the prompt in this format:

```markdown
> **Copy everything below into your AI Chat.** Press Enter. Wait for the output.

<!-- prompt: v2.0 — Generated by Jungle Prompter -->
```yaml
role: <generated-role>
tools: [<required-tools>]
instruction: |
  <instruction tailored to their specific Jungle Title and current task>
```
```

**Example Output for "Mechanic (D2)":**

```markdown
> **Copy everything below into your AI Chat.** Press Enter. Wait for the output.

<!-- prompt: v2.0 — Generated by Jungle Prompter -->
```yaml
role: sdet-helper
tools: [filesystem, playwright]
instruction: |
  You are my SDET assistant.
  I have a failing test: [Paste error here].
  1. Analyze `mock/index.html` to find the correct `data-testid` for the element.
  2. Rewrite the failing step to use the correct selector and add a `waitFor()` condition.
  3. Explain why the original selector failed.
```

# 📋 Participant Data Consent Form

## King of the AI Jungle Workshop — Data Processing Consent

> **Document Purpose:** This form establishes the lawful basis for collecting, storing, and using participant data during the King of the AI Jungle workshop, in compliance with GDPR Article 6(1).

---

## 1. Data Collection Scope

The following types of data may be collected during the workshop:

| Data Type | Description | Collection Method |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Workshop Artifacts** | Test plans, risk analyses, automation scripts, and other deliverables produced during the exercise | Self-submitted by participant |
| **Session Photos/Screenshots** | Screenshots of work progress, debug sessions, or shared outputs | Participant-captured or instructor-collected |
| **Feedback Responses** | Answers to workshop evaluation questions (Likert scales, open text) | Digital form or paper survey |
| **AI Chat Logs** | Prompts submitted to the AI model and responses received | Automatic capture in BUDGET-LOG.md |
| **Participant Information** | Name, role, and Jungle Title assignment (for facilitation only) | Self-reported at Day-0 check-in |

## 2. Purpose of Processing

Data collected during this workshop is processed **only** for the following purposes:

1. **Workshop Evaluation:** Assessing the effectiveness of the workshop content and delivery.
2. **Learning Assessment:** Evaluating participant progress and skill development.
3. **Instructor Improvement:** Identifying areas for improvement in the workshop materials and facilitation.
4. **Compliance Documentation:** Maintaining records that the workshop was conducted in accordance with data protection requirements.

**Data will NOT be used for:** Performance evaluation, employment decisions, marketing, or any purpose outside the workshop.

## 3. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Disposal Method |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| Workshop Artifacts | 30 days post-workshop | Secure deletion (shred/overwrite) |
| Session Photos/Screenshots | 30 days post-workshop | Secure deletion |
| Feedback Responses | 30 days post-workshop | Secure deletion |
| AI Chat Logs | 30 days post-workshop | BUDGET-LOG.md entries removed |
| Participant Information | 30 days post-workshop | Secure deletion |

After the retention period, all data will be securely deleted. A data retention log will be maintained to track deletion dates.

## 4. Participant Rights

Under GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

- **Right of Access:** Request a copy of all data collected about you during the workshop.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of any inaccurate personal data.
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your data at any time (before or after the workshop).
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Request that your data processing be limited.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object:** Object to the processing of your data for evaluation purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the workshop instructor at the contact information provided below.

## 5. Data Sharing

Your data will **NOT** be shared with any third party. The only exception is:

- **Instructor Review:** Workshop instructors may review your artifacts and feedback as part of the learning assessment described in Section 2.

No data is transmitted to external services, cloud platforms, or third-party analytics tools.

## 6. Data Security

All workshop data is stored:
- **Locally** on the instructor's and participants' machines (within the `King-of-the-AI-Jungle/` project directory).
- **Encrypted** at rest where supported by the operating system.
- **Access-controlled** — only workshop instructors and designated reviewers can access the data.

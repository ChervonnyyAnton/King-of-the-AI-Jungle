## 📐 Risk Assessment
- **Risk Score:** 3 × 3 = **9** → **Medium**
- **Probability:** Possible (3) — instructors without facilitation training may mismatch energy to the segment.
- **Impact:** Affects all paths. Wrong energy level during a segment reduces learning effectiveness — participants are disengaged during the Showdown or distracted during the Sprint.

## 🔄 R&R Rule Derivation
This cycle produced **Rule PW-1** in Section 19 of `R&R-CYCLE-RULES.md`:
> **Rule PW-1 — Pacing & Energy Management:** The workshop delivery flow must use a 3-tier energy model (Calm/Focus, Balanced/Active, High/Energetic) mapped to each segment. Instructors must match their voice, movement, and interaction style to the segment's energy level. Showdown segments → High/Energetic; Independent Sprint → Calm/Focus; Theory, Demo, Peer Review → Balanced/Active. No single energy level should persist for more than 30 consecutive minutes to prevent participant fatigue.

# 🔄 Review & Remediation: Cycle 161 — Gamification: Jungle Badge Taxonomy

## 🎯 Objective
Define the taxonomy of "Jungle Badges" — a set of achievement badges awarded to participants based on their behavior during the workshop. This addresses the absence of any gamification layer in the current workshop design.

## 📋 Findings & Remediation

| Area | Finding | Severity | 2-Axis Impact | Remediation |
|------|---------|----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Gamification** | No badge system exists in any workshop artifact. Participants complete tasks but receive no immediate visual recognition for specific behaviors (e.g., token efficiency, thorough debugging). This reduces engagement, especially for Jungle Titles that rely on competitive framing ("King of the Jungle"). | **Medium** | **All Paths:** The "King of the Jungle" theme is inherently competitive; gamification is thematically appropriate for all 24 Jungle Titles. Without badges, the theme is only in the title, not in the experience. | Added **7 Jungle Badges** to `WORKSHOP-CHALLENGE.md` (Step 3b: Gamification) with emoji icons, names, unlock criteria, and point values. |

## 📂 Updated Files
- **`WORKSHOP-CHALLENGE.md`**: Added "Step 3b: Jungle Badges" section with 7 badge definitions.

## 📐 Risk Assessment
- **Risk Score:** 2 × 3 = **6** → **Medium**
- **Probability:** Possible (2) — participants may not engage with badges without instructor guidance.
- **Impact:** Medium — badges enhance engagement but are not required for core workshop delivery.

## 🔄 R&R Rule Derivation

> **Rule GAM-1 — Jungle Badge System:** The workshop must include a gamified badge system that awards participants for specific, observable behaviors. At minimum, the following badges must be defined: Bug Hunter, Token Saver, Debug King, Quality Champion, Speed Runner, Team Player, and Jungle Explorer. Each badge has a name, emoji icon, unlock criteria (≤2 sentences), and point value (5–20 points). Badges are announced publicly during the Showdown.
